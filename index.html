FAQs

Questions, answered.

Is the estimate a quote?

No. It is a planning estimate. An official quote verifies the retailer, shipping and destination charges.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Add the number of identical items you want. We’ll treat them as one combined shipment where practical.

What about import tax?

We use destination tax profiles to provide an indicative planning figure. Actual treatment depends on the goods and import arrangement.

Do I have to create an account?

No. You can get an estimate without creating an account or giving us your email.
