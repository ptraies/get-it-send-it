FAQs

A few sensible questions.

Is the estimate exact?

No. It is a planning estimate. Shipping, customs classification, local taxes and retailer charges can change the final amount.

Why is shipping a range?

Because the final carrier price can depend on weight, dimensions, packaging, service level and destination. We would rather show a sensible window than invent a false single figure.

What happens next?

When you need a firm figure, request an official quote. That's where the retailer, carrier and destination charges are checked in detail.

What about heavy items?

Where a reliable weight can be established, the planning range can be adjusted. Very heavy or specialist shipments should move to a bespoke quote rather than a guessed number.

What does the service fee cover?

The service fee is the greater of £15 or 15% of the product price only. It does not increase because shipping or local taxes are high.

Will tax rules keep changing?

Yes. That's why the planning engine and its tax research need regular review rather than being treated as a one-off database.
